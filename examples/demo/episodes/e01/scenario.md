# Episode 1 (demo)

## s01 Lena's dream: the door
- characters: Lena
- location:
- duration: 6
Medium shot from behind. Lena, in an evening dress, opens a heavy iron door and stands at the threshold of a dim basement room. The concrete walls are painted green up to chest height, with white plaster above, like in a cellar. The paint is old and peeling. The door is gloomy. Dull yellow light. We never see her face.

## s02 Lena's dream: the darkroom
- characters: Lena
- location:
- duration: 5
Inside an old photo darkroom, 1980. Red safelight, as used for developing film. Film negatives hang to dry on strings. Around her are shelves with jars, trays and photo-printing equipment. There are no digital cameras here, only film. Lena finds photographs on a table, picks them up and stares at them, shocked, as if they frighten her.
