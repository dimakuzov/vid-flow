A dim Soviet basement corridor of 1980, seen at eye level from about two metres in front of a heavy iron door that stands in the centre of the far wall. The lower half of the concrete walls is painted in dull bottle-green oil paint, chipped and peeling in patches down to bare grey concrete, and above a sharp painted line the upper walls are rough white plaster stained with damp. The iron door is dark and riveted, with a heavy handle and rusty hinges, its paint flaking, and it stands slightly ajar onto darkness. A bare bulb in a wire cage on the ceiling gives a weak dull yellow light that makes a pale yellow pool on the grey concrete floor and soft shadows in the corners. A rusty pipe runs along the wall beside the door and a coil of old cable hangs from a hook. Empty room, no people. Photorealistic, 35mm lens, eye level, wide enough to show the whole wall with the door, sharp focus from front to back, muted film colors.
