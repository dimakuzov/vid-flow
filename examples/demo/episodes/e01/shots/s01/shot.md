# Lena's dream: the door

## Summary

Medium shot from behind. Lena, in an evening dress, opens a heavy iron door and stands at the threshold of a dim basement room. The concrete walls are painted green up to chest height, with white plaster above, like in a cellar. The paint is old and peeling. The door is gloomy. Dull yellow light. We never see her face.
